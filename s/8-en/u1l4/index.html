C — Use Aunt Sally’s notes to write her advice. Write one paragraph to each person.
agree, not right – don’t give h.w. – v. unhelpful – not helping your friend – doesn’t do own work, won’t learn anything – e.g., can’t copy in exams – explain
✅ الناتج: I agree. It is not right. Don’t give your homework to your friend. You are not helping her. If she doesn’t do her own work, she won’t learn anything. She can’t copy in exams. Explain this to her. I hope she understands.
السؤال D (1)
D — Read these problems. Use Aunt Sally’s notes to write her advice.
Problem: I stammer when the teacher asks me a question. Notes: count to 5 before u spk. – think abt. what to say – ask yr. teacher to help – she’ll understand
✅ الناتج: Why not count to five before you speak? Think about what you want to say. You can also ask your teacher to help. She will understand.
السؤال D (2)
D — Read these problems. Use Aunt Sally’s notes to write her advice.
Problem: I cannot remember new words in my French class. Notes: write words in groups in yr. vocabulary bk. e.g. names of food, buildings, clothes – ask for help – learn 5 new words a day
✅ الناتج: Write new words in groups in your vocabulary book, for example, names of food, buildings or clothes. Ask your brother or sister for help. Try to learn five new words every day.
