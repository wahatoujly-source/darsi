السؤال A
Complete: lighthouses, drums, pigeons, Damascus — 1 In Africa, people used ____ to send messages. 2 The capital city of Syria is ____. 3 ____ warn ships of danger. 4 ____ can fly from Cairo to Damascus.
✅ الناتج: 1 drums ، 2 Damascus ، 3 Lighthouses ، 4 Pigeons
السؤال B
Write about getting and sending messages and information. Follow the plan.
✅ الناتج: In the past, it was difficult to get and to send messages and information. Now it is easy. It is easy to find out what is happening in your country and in other countries. You can go to a news website and read the news, or watch the news on television. It is also easy to get special information, for example about a famous person. You can look on the internet or read a magazine. Most people like to know what their friends are doing, even if they live in another country. They can send messages, emails or use social media.
