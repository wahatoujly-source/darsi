السؤال A (1)
There was a young ____ from ____, / Who got to ____ every day ____. / When his ____ asked ‘____’ / He replied with a ____, / ‘My ____ always wakes me at ____.’
✅ الناتج: There was a young boy from Kuwait, / Who got to school every day late. / When his teacher asked ‘Why?’ / He replied with a cry, / ‘My clock always wakes me at eight.’

✅ الناتج: There was an old man from Jaipur, / Whose house had a green and blue door. / I said, ‘I like white.’ / He said, ‘That’s all right. / But I think that white is a bore.’
