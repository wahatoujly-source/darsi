السؤال A
Complete the conversations: right, wetlands, you’re wrong, disagree, sea, agree
✅ الناتج: 1 I disagree. Blue whales live in the sea. ، 2 I think you’re wrong. Golden toads live in the wetlands. ، 3 I agree. You’re right.
السؤال B (1)
B — Are the sentences true (T) or false (F)?
People are still allowed to hunt blue whales.
✅ الناتج: F — Blue whales are now protected.
السؤال B (2)
B — Are the sentences true (T) or false (F)?
Male and female golden toads are different colours.
✅ الناتج: T
السؤال B (3)
B — Are the sentences true (T) or false (F)?
People hunted tigers because they are very dangerous.
✅ الناتج: F — They hunted them for their skins.
السؤال C
Either agree or disagree with these statements (A We don’t need wild animals. B We should help poor people. C Football is the best game. D Fish is better than meat. E We don’t need plastic bags.)
✅ الناتج: A I disagree, because wild animals are very interesting to see. ، B I agree. It’s unfair that some people don’t have enough. ، C I disagree. Tennis is more interesting. ، D I disagree. Eating some meat is good. ، E I agree. We should use paper bags.
